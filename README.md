## What Is Clouvider?

Clouvider is a UK-based hosting provider that does VPS, dedicated servers, private cloud, and colocation. They operate out of 11 data centres spread across the UK, Europe, and the US — London, Manchester, Amsterdam, Frankfurt, New York, Ashburn, Atlanta, Chicago, Dallas, Phoenix, and Los Angeles.

Their infrastructure runs on Supermicro enterprise servers with Intel Xeon and AMD EPYC processors, and NVMe storage throughout. The network backbone is Juniper-powered with 9 Tbps+ total capacity, and they peer directly at major IXes including LINX, AMS-IX, DE-CIX Frankfurt, and others. Transit is via Level3/Lumen, Telia/Arelion, GTT, and TATA.

The short version: they're genuinely infrastructure-heavy for a mid-market host. It's not just marketing.

**The network quality is the main selling point.** In community circles where people benchmark obsessively, Clouvider comes up as one of a small handful of providers with a legitimately premium network. No Cogent (which some users feel strongly about), clean routing to Europe and the US West Coast, and good cross-Atlantic performance.

**Things to know before buying:** The service is unmanaged by default, which is exactly what experienced operators want but could be a headache for beginners. Port 25 (SMTP) is blocked on VPS plans — fine for almost everyone, but worth knowing if you're planning to run a mail server. Additional IPv4 addresses cost extra. No Windows license is provided (but you can bring your own ISO and install it yourself).
